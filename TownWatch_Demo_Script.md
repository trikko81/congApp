# TownWatch Demo Video Script

**Target length:** about 90 seconds
**Before recording:** start the app, open it in a browser, and make sure the local API is running. Record your screen and voice. Keep the cursor movements slow.

## Screen and narration

### 1. Home screen — 10 seconds

**Show:** TownWatch home screen.

**Say:**

> Hi, I’m [your name]. This is TownWatch, a web app that helps people find and understand information from local government documents.

### 2. Civic Feed — 20 seconds

**Show:** Click **Civic Feed**. Scroll to one item and open it. Point out its summary and source citation.

**Say:**

> Local agendas and meeting packets can be long and hard to follow. The Civic Feed organizes public issues into topics and gives each item a short explanation, so people can quickly see what it’s about.

### 3. PDF evidence — 15 seconds

**Show:** Open the cited PDF page in the viewer. Pause so the page is readable.

**Say:**

> TownWatch connects the explanation to its source. I can open the cited PDF page and check the original document myself.

### 4. Your area — 25 seconds

**Show:** Click **Your area**. Search **Virginia Beach**. Show the HB52 result and the “Open cited PDF page 3” button. Open it if the PDF is available.

**Say:**

> The Your area section searches enacted Virginia bills for an explicit mention of a locality. For Virginia Beach, this result is House Bill 52, Chapter 908, and the mention appears on page 3. A city being named is a useful lead; the bill’s wording determines who is actually covered.

### 5. Close — 15 seconds

**Show:** Return to the app home screen.

**Say:**

> I built TownWatch to make public information easier to find and verify. I’d like to expand it with more local documents and help residents discover the issues that matter to them. Thanks for watching.

## Quick recording checklist

- Replace **[your name]** with your name.
- Close unrelated tabs and notifications; zoom the browser so text is easy to read.
- Do one practice run and confirm the Civic Feed, PDF viewer, and Your area search work.
- Keep the video public and submit a link that starts with `https`.
